# M1.5 — BotLogic IRC channel roles

**M1.5 — PASS.**

Engo's optional BotLogic state model now observes IRC channel role MODE changes and publishes:

- `channel_operator(Channel, Nick)` for `+o/-o`;
- `voiced(Channel, Nick)` for `+v/-v`.

Combined mode strings such as `+ov Alice Bob` are parsed parameter-by-parameter.

Role state follows the membership lifecycle:

- NICK moves known role facts to the new nick;
- PART removes known roles in the parted channel;
- KICK removes known roles for the victim in that channel;
- QUIT removes known roles from every indexed channel.

## Authority boundary

Channel operator and voice facts describe observed IRC channel state. They do not replace Engo's verified account identity or local command permission system and cannot independently grant Engo administrative privileges.

The feed remains structured, optional and fail-open.
