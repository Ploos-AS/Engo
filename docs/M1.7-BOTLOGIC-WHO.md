# M1.7 — BotLogic WHO enrichment

**M1.7 — PASS.**

After joining configured channels, Engo now sends a standard IRC `WHO <channel>` request.

Standard `352 RPL_WHOREPLY` responses enrich the optional BotLogic model with:

- `channel_member(Channel, Nick)`;
- `online(Nick)`.

This complements the NAMES bootstrap and can repair membership state from a second standard IRC source.

## Account provenance boundary

Standard RFC-style WHO replies do not contain a services-account field. Engo therefore does **not** infer or assert `authenticated(Nick, Account)` from `352` user, host, server, flags or real-name fields.

Verified account state continues to come from explicit IRCv3 account sources such as account-notify, account-tag and extended-join where provenance checks succeed.

WHOX is intentionally not guessed in this milestone. A future extension may negotiate/use a specific WHOX field token and parser with deterministic tests.

The BotLogic integration remains optional, structured and fail-open.
